Your ledger lives on this device

This data is not stored online. If you open this website on another device or browser, it will create a new, empty ledger.

To keep your finances safe and move them between devices, click Encrypted backup and download a backup file. Do it regularly.

  1. Download an encrypted backup.
  2. On the other device, choose Restore backup.
  3. Enter the original password for that backup.
